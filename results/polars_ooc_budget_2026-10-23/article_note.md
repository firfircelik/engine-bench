Third update (Oct 23)

Ritchie Vink, the creator of Polars, pointed out that only DuckDB was told about the memory limit, so Polars did not know it had to stay under 4 GiB, and suggested setting POLARS_OOC_MEMORY_BUDGET_MB for a fairer comparison. I reran every Polars query at 150M rows on the same box under the same 4.5 GiB cap with that budget, next to a DuckDB rerun in the same session: the high-cardinality group by finished 3/3 at 6.17 s and 3.46 GiB with a 3072 MB budget, against 9.56 s for DuckDB (the box was busier than on Oct 22 and DuckDB ran slower too, so compare within this session, not with the tables above). The window dedup with over(order_by) still went over the cap in every run at 3072, 1024 and 512 MB, Victor's sort plus unique version fit only at 1024 MB (31.28 s, 2.29 GiB), and the max-timestamp rewrite finished 3/3 on default settings (5.61 s, 4.02 GiB). Ritchie says Polars main now spills the group by correctly and that this ships in the next release, which was not on PyPI yet when I ran this, so I will rerun when it ships. Raw results and output checks are in <PR_URL> and in results/polars_ooc_budget_2026-10-23 on the polars-ooc-budget-2026-10-23 branch.
